Boardgames

3D clones of my favourite boardgames, built in the Godot game engine with Claude Code. They are for personal use only, so each game asks for a password.


Lords of Waterdeep

Lords of Waterdeep
Date Created: October 2026
A 3D clone of the Dungeons & Dragons worker-placement boardgame. Play against three CPU opponents and choose each one's skill level (Okay, Average or Good) at the start of a game.
Give it a moment to load the first time, as the card art takes a while to download.
