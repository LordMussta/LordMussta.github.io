8 September 2026
Finished and published Castle Explorer, a full 2D top-down exploration game built in Godot 4.7. It's my fourth attempt at the "Castle of Doom" story I first wrote back in 2018 - this time with a proper engine behind it: a hand-built room/corridor layout, wandering NPCs, a furniture "decorate mode", and a small zoo of enemy creatures. It exports straight to WebGL, so it's playable right in the browser. Almost all of it was built pairing with Claude Code, which freed me up to focus on design decisions (door placement, collision sizes, sprite scale) instead of engine boilerplate.

25 August 2026
Lost a weekend to Warcraft III: Reforged (Legacy client) refusing to launch cleanly through Lutris/Wine, and to its cutscenes playing back in the wrong screen quadrant. Turned out to be two unrelated bugs - a launch crash, and a genuine upstream bug in Wine's video renderer that needed an actual patch to Wine itself. Wrote the whole investigation up in the repo in case it saves someone else the same weekend.
Repo: Warcraft3_Legacy_Wine_VideoFix

29 January 2022
Spent a frustrating but educational evening getting an old version of my portfolio site running on Heroku. The fix turned out to be two small things: Heroku expects your entry file to be called app.py (because the Procfile says app:app), and I'd left host='0.0.0.0' in app.run() from developing on my iPad. Fixed both, actually read heroku logs --tail properly this time, and it deployed fine.
